Courses & staff

Which advanced courses a high school offers, AP, calculus, physics, chemistry, computer science, dual enrollment and more, how many students take them, and its counselors and security staff. About 1 in 4 reported no AP students and no calculus or physics classes in the 2023-24 federal collection, a count that includes alternative, special-education and juvenile-justice schools.
