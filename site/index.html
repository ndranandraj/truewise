New finding

1,932 of 4,635 colleges had not submitted at least one required FVT/GE file, as recorded in ED’s list compiled on 6 August 2026. “Submitted” does not mean “complete”.

What a result looks like

1 in 10 undergraduate programs with published earnings have graduates who earn less than a typical high-school graduate.

4,308 of 41,198 certificate, associate’s and bachelor’s programs, each counted once. Only about 28% of programs can be judged; ED publishes no earnings for most of the rest. Where only one-year earnings are published, those are used; on four-year figures alone it is 1 in 13. Cosmetology: 96% fall short.

How we stay honest

  • Every figure carries its source and date.
  • Nothing is estimated to fill a gap. When ED publishes no figure, the page says so.
  • Earnings describe past graduates. They are not a promise.
