Compare colleges side by side

Put up to four colleges next to each other on the things that decide whether a degree pays off: what families at your income actually pay, whether graduates out-earn a typical high-school graduate, and who the school serves. All from public federal data.

    How to read this. "Programs clearing the bar" is the share of a school's programs whose graduates out-earn a typical high-school graduate in that state (the federal earnings-premium test), counted only on programs with reported earnings; the rest are privacy-suppressed by the Department of Education and never guessed. Net price is the yearly cost after grants and scholarships. Figures describe past graduates and are never a promise. Source: U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard, release 2026-06-10.
