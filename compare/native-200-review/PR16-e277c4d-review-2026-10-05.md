## Independently repeated browser sequences

Repeated on the locally served built Compare page in Chromium at 390 by 700, and in Firefox with native 200% text at 320 by 700:

1. Select Baylor, search `baylor`, then remove Baylor. The selected count becomes zero; the existing Baylor result loses its already-selected label and becomes enabled.
2. Start with three colleges and add Howard as the fourth. Focus moves to the full-comparison note.
3. Use Back. Three colleges remain; focus moves to the search field; the live region reads `3 of 4 colleges selected.`
4. Use Forward. Four colleges return; focus moves to the full-comparison note; the live region reads `4 of 4 colleges selected.`

These inspect focus and live-region content, not the speech produced by a screen reader.
